## 🤖 CT CLAUDE · Turn 55 · 2026-11-11

I couldn't find an open source project that does the whole job. Several cover one piece of it, the export step, and none handle the rest. This is from one round of searching, so it isn't exhaustive. Your last turn was 43, so I've numbered this 44. Correct me if that's wrong.

### What exists

- **Anthropic's built-in export.** Claude.ai has a data export under Settings > Privacy, which gives a raw JSON dump of your whole history. It is a batch export, not live logging. A community tool, claude-export-viewer, turns that ZIP into a browsable static HTML site.
- **Browser extensions.** Claude Exporter by agoramachina is MIT-licensed and works in Chrome and Firefox. Chat Export by Trifall exports Claude and ChatGPT conversations to Markdown, XML, JSON and HTML, and says it stores nothing remotely.
- **Console scripts.** claude-chat-exporter is a JavaScript tool you run in the browser console to produce a Markdown file. The ryanschiang script does the same for Markdown, JSON or PNG, but it was last confirmed working in September 2024, so the Claude page may have changed since.

### What none of them do

These are the parts we've been designing, and I found nothing that covers them:

- Per-project routing, so a shortcut writes to the right repo.
- Session start and close markers.
- Turn numbering that continues across sessions.
- A commit that fails loudly.
- The privacy check before push.

They export a conversation. They don't manage a logging workflow.

### Caution before installing any of them

The fast.io comparison I found warns that four extensions passed Google's review while harvesting AI conversations from millions of users. It also notes that the most popular exporter, with over 70,000 installs, doesn't publish its source code. Your logs go to a public repo, but your private survey answers and chats don't. An extension that can read claude.ai can read all of it. Prefer open-source ones you've read, or the console scripts.

### My suggestion

Keep the design as it is. The clipboard step is the simplest part, and the new work is the routing, numbering and safety layer around it. That layer is where an existing tool would help least.

An exporter could still replace the per-turn copy later, since it can capture a whole conversation at once. Its output would then need reformatting to your heading convention, and it would add an extension to your trust list. By your five questions, that doesn't solve a problem you have today. I'd park it, and revisit it if copying turn by turn becomes the pain point.

I haven't tested any of these tools or checked their output against your log format. Say if you want me to compare one properly.
