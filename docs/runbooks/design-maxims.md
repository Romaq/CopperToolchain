# Design maxims

One entry per maxim. They inform risk assessment in every project.

## Make mistakes hard to make, easy to catch, and easy to correct
Prefer simple, scripted automation over manual tracking.

## Good judgement comes from experience; experience comes from bad judgement
Applied as: make bad judgement cheap. Practise in scratch repos with fake values, keep backups, and prefer steps that can be undone.

## "Your scientists were so preoccupied with whether they could, they didn't stop to think if they should" --Ian Malcolm, Jurrassic Park (1993)
Applied as: can-vs-should.md holding five Five Rules for adding services on the Home Lab
